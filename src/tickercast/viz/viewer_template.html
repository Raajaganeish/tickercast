Predicted range vs what really happened

Each trading day the model gives a range for the next day's change: a low (only a 10% chance the stock falls further), a middle guess, and a high (only a 10% chance it rises more). The dots are what actually happened. A good forecast keeps about 8 in 10 dots inside the band and widens before big moves. None of these days were used to train or tune the models.
